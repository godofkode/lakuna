Process

How Lakuna works

01

Start on frontier

All traffic begins on the frontier model. Nothing moves until it's proven safe to move.

02

Map real workloads

Actual requests get clustered into work-types, not synthetic benchmarks.

03

Qualify candidates

Deterministic verification plus selective A/B testing scores each model against each cluster.

04

Migrate traffic

Only clusters that clear the quality bar move to local models — progressively, and measured the whole way.

Rationale

Why this, why now

Routing research shows complementary models beat any single strongest model. Lakuna packages the measurement-and-transition system that serious teams currently stitch together by hand.

Trust before traffic

No traffic moves to a local model until the matrix says it's earned it.

Cheap to maintain

Only affected cells get invalidated when a model, prompt, or runtime changes.

A learning loop

The matrix shows exactly where local models fall short — pointing straight at what to train next.
