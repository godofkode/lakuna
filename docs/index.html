Process

How Lakuna works

01

Start on frontier

All traffic begins on the frontier model. Nothing moves until it's proven safe to move.

02

Map real workloads

Actual requests get clustered into work-types, not synthetic benchmarks.

03

Qualify local candidates

Deterministic verification plus selective A/B testing scores each model against each cluster.

04

Migrate qualified traffic

Only clusters that clear the quality bar move to local models — progressively, and measured the whole way.

Rationale

Why this, why now
